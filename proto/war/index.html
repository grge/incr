Skirmish

Cells fill with charge and fire into their neighbours. Your charge cancels the rival's. Take the land, take the nodes.

  • holdyour land to grow a well. The longer you hold, the stronger it pulls. A well swallows nearby cells that aren't yours, charge and all.
  • tapyour well to burst it: the cells it swallowed are thrown out over the front.
  • dragyour well to move it.
  • tapyour node to focus your flow through it.

Your flow grows with the cells you own. Each node you hold gives 2 pull for wells. A well breaks if the rival holds the ground under it. Win with 80% of the cells, or by taking every node the rival has.
